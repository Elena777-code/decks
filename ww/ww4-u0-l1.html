SB p. 6 · ex. 2 · read again — true or false?

The text — true or false?

Bea Baxter is sixteen. Bea and her cousin Abe Kerr live in New Park, near London. They usually get on really well and spend a lot of time together.

Abe’s good at photography and his new hobby is baking. He likes hiking and cycling. His friend Eren King loves tennis and enjoys cycling with Abe although he sometimes has problems with his bike.

Bea and her best friend Carla Silva are classmates. They aren’t neighbours, but Carla lives in the next street, so they often see each other. Carla likes acting and art. She has First Aid classes every week too. Bea doesn’t do First Aid classes — she prefers to do gardening and programming in her free time.

Bea and Carla sometimes play frisbee with Abe and Eren in a local park. They usually have fun. The park’s a great place to relax and they all like hanging out there.

1Bea and her cousin live in the same town. → T
2Abe and Bea often argue. → F — they usually get on really well
3Eren and Abe don’t enjoy any of the same things. → F — they both enjoy cycling
4Carla lives near Bea. → T
5Carla and Bea have similar interests. → F — acting and art vs gardening and programming
6All the friends enjoy spending time in the park. → T

GRAMMAR A · Present Simple, adverbs of frequency, wh- questions

Present Simple

+I live in London.  ·  She likes acting.
−I don’t like doing sports.  ·  She doesn’t do First Aid classes.
?Do you live near your cousins? — Yes, I do./No, I don’t.  ·  Does she go to your school? — Yes, she does./No, she doesn’t.
advalways, never, usually, often, rarely, sometimes

Wh- questions

1Where do you live?

2When do you see friends?

3Who do you hang out with?

4Which school do you go to?

5Why do you like your home?

6What sports do you play?

7How often do you visit your relatives?

SB p. 7 · ex. 7 · track 0.3 · the dialogue

The timetable — like or dislike?

🎧 0.3 Read the dialogue. What subjects and classes do Abe and Bea like/dislike?

Abe: Have you got your timetable for this term yet?

Bea: Yes, I’m actually looking forward to Monday mornings! I have Biology first and then Information Technology.

Abe: Why do you like Biology so much? It’s the worst science subject!

Bea: Biology’s fun — and it’s easy. I have to say IT is the best, though. I’d love to do some more programming. I might do a science discovery project this year too.

Abe: Sometimes I just don’t understand you!

Bea: Well, that’s because you’re really good at Art and creative subjects.

Abe: True. Hey, do you want to try some after-school classes?

Bea: Yeah, why not? Let’s have a look at the list.

Abe: What about First Aid?

Bea: You’re joking! I need to do something more relaxing after school.

Abe: We can join Drama Club.

Bea: Hmm … I guess it’s better than chess or football.

Abelikes … / dislikes … → likes: Art, creative subjects, Drama · dislikes: Biology
Bealikes … / dislikes … → likes: Biology and IT · dislikes: chess and football
