Grammar · SB p. 108 · questions — the four shapes · + Bright sparks in three facts

Questions — the four shapes

1 · Yes/No — inversion
You are hungry. → Are you hungry? I enjoyed it. → Did you enjoy it?

2 · Wh- — question word + inversion
Why are you hungry? When does she cycle?

3 · Subject questions — who/what IS the subject: no do
Who taught you? What happened?

4 · Object questions — who/what is the object: do + subject
Who did they see? — They saw Tommo. What did you buy?
