Cards ‘Sentences’ · find the mistake · 4 minutes

Pick a card — correct your partner!

In pairs: read a sentence to your partner — they correct the mistake and say the correct sentence. Then swap. 4 minutes.

Sheet 1

1I’ve already finished my project. → ✓ correct
2She isn’t usually taking the bus to work. → She doesn’t usually take the bus to work.
3We haven’t discussed this problem last week. → We didn’t discuss this problem last week.
4Dan is knowing a lot about IT work … → Dan knows a lot about IT work … (stative!)

Worksheet ‘Rewrite the sentences’ · 5 minutes

Same idea — new grammar form

Rewrite the sentences — change the grammar form named in brackets (and its time marker!). Alone — 5 minutes, then check in groups of 3–4.

1I usually travel by train. (Present Continuous — today) → I’m travelling by train today.
2She is watching a stream right now. (Present Simple — habit) → She watches streams every day.
3We filmed a video in the park two days ago. (Present Perfect) → We have filmed a video in the park.
4Uncle Fred works late on Fridays. (Past Simple — last Friday) → Uncle Fred worked late last Friday.
5My parents have already booked the tickets. (Past Simple — yesterday) → My parents booked the tickets yesterday.
6I studied for the exam yesterday evening. (Present Perfect) → I have studied for the exam.
7She has lived abroad for three years. (Past Simple — finished situation) → She lived abroad for three years.
8We don’t usually eat out on weekdays. (Present Continuous — temporary situation) → We aren’t eating out on weekdays this month.
9They are planning a trip at the moment. (Present Perfect) → They have planned a trip.
