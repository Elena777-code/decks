bi = 2 … but three chips?!

Three moves: ① where the chips start → ② the +1 climb → ③ the rule. Tap each line!

million is the 1st step of the ladder — and it already carries two chips: 1000000
One thousand × one thousand = two chips of zeros. Count them: 2!

Every new word means «a thousand of the previous word».
A thousand millions = billion → add one more chip: 3 chips.
A thousand billions = trillion → 4 chips. The ladder climbs like that forever ↗

The Latin number counts STEPS — not chips!
chips = steps + 1  ·  zeros = chips × 3

the ladder · step 2

billion

1000000000

🔍 the root

bi = TWO (Latin). Billion = the second step after million.

🚲 bicycle — two wheels🗣️ bilingual — two languages💻 binary — two digits: 0 and 1👓 binoculars — for two eyes

🔢 the zeros

9 zeros — three chips. Short way: 10⁹

🇷🇺 the Russian twin

Russian says миллиард — a different word, the SAME number. And the music word: секунда — the 2nd step in music, and «the second» small part of a minute! 🎵

⚠️ the look-alike trap

библия and библиотека LOOK like bi-… but no! Their root is Greek biblos = book (from the city of Byblos, which sold papyrus 📜). And библиотека and аптека share -тека — Greek for «a chest where things are kept». Books and medicines — both live in their -тека!

🤯 wow

One billion seconds = about 32 years. A person who is 32 has lived a billion seconds!

the ladder · step 4

quadrillion

1000000000000000

🔍 the root

quad / quart = FOUR (Latin quartus — fourth). The fourth step.

🪙 quarter — one fourth (¼)×4 quadruple

🔢 the zeros

15 zeros — five chips. Short way: 10¹⁵

🇷🇺 the Russian family

⬜ квадрат🏍 квадроцикл 🎻 квартет🏙 квартал 🎵 кварта

A square has four sides; a квадроцикл has four wheels; a квартет is four musicians; a квартал is a quarter — of a year or a city; кварта is the 4th step in music.

🏠 and… квартира?

YES — a distant true cousin! The word travelled: Latin quartus (fourth) → German Quartier — a soldier's quarter, one-fourth share of a house → Russian квартира. Your home secretly means «a fourth»! 🏠

🤯 wow

A quadrillion seconds = 31 million years — longer than there have been humans on Earth 👣

🏛️ the story

Long ago, Rome's year began in MARCH — September really was month 7. Later, two new months — January and February — were added at the front. Every month slid two places down… but the names never changed. The calendar moved; the words froze. ❄️

✨ beyond the ladder

googol

10^100
…that's 1 with a hundred zeros — the digits would not fit on this slide!

👦 the boy who named it

In 1920, a mathematician asked his nephew — a boy of NINE, exactly your age — to invent a name for 1-with-a-hundred-zeros. The boy said… «googol!» 😄

🔎 the misspelling

The website Google is «googol» spelled wrong — the founders liked the mistake and kept it!

🤯 wow

A googol is bigger than the number of atoms in everything you can see in the sky 🌌

🌀 and bigger still

A googolplex = 1 + a googol zeros. There is not enough room in the universe to write it out. Not even close.

✍️ the shortest way to write giants

Powers of ten

10⁰= 1
the little number up high = how many zeros

✅ worked out

3.5 billion = 3.5 × 10⁹ — because a billion carries nine zeros. The zeros count becomes the little number!

✅ your turn to see it

7 trillion = 7 × 10? — a trillion has twelve zeros → 7 × 10¹². That's ALL the trick 🎩
