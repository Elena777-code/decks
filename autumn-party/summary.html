The idea

90 minutes — English by making

No sitting still: every stage makes the children speak, guess or cook
0:00Знакомство — welcome games "We are Team…"
0:08Страшилка — a Halloween story or video 5 minutes, lights low
0:15Хэллоуин-квиз — the quiz, with the holiday's history "Is it TRUE or SPOOKY?"
0:30Игра на лексику — the words game sweet · sour · spooky · crunchy
0:45Карамельные яблоки — cooking in teams "Let's dip! It needs more…"
1:15Чаепитие — tea + the talk "My favourite part was…"
The team

Who does what

Elena the English

Leads the language, the games and the quiz. Media & logistics on top. This week: the programme, the form, Дина.

Alice the kitchen

Venue, recipes and their tests, the food ideas, shopping, adapting to allergies. The sweets menu is hers.

Дина the door

Brings the audience (her pupils) and helps with a location. Her son takes part. Her percentage is a budget line.

Вера print & pack

Prints and laminates the cards, labels the boxes, packs everything for the day.

Ksenia camera & clock

On the day: timing ("five minutes left!") and filming the 10-shot list. Faces only with consent.

open food & safety

One calm adult owning hands, knives, the stove and the cleaning. Дина — a chemistry teacher — is the natural candidate. To confirm.

The money frame

The budget — five lines to fill with Дина

1 · Д's percentage — her share for the audience and the location discuss

2 · Shopping — apples, caramel, cookies, icing, tea, decorations

3 · Workers' pay — two people on the day

4 · Organisers' pay — two people (Elena, Alice)

5 · The headcount — the number everything is divided by

Price per child = all of the above, divided, plus the margin — parents pay slightly less if they join the cooking (the meeting's decision)

The parents — decided at the meeting

Four rules for the form

1. Желательно без родителей — preferably without parents in the room

2. Родители могут участвовать в приготовлении, если хотят — parents may join the cooking if they want

3. Нужно знать заранее, сколько будет родителей — we need the parents' number in advance

4. Оплата родителей чуть меньше — a joining parent pays slightly less

All four go into the registration form → deck 2 · Invitation & registration
