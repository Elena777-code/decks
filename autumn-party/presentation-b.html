English they actually used

1

Five new words — taste, food, Halloween — used out loud, not memorised

2

A prediction — "I think it will harden because…"

3

Kitchen requests — "Could you pass me…?", "How much do we add?"

4

A 20–40 second pitch — "First…, then…, finally…" about their team's apple

5

One question asked to another team — in English, on their own

6

safe & tidy

food

Allergies collected at registration; the full ingredient list goes to every family in advance; nobody is made to taste anything.

kitchen

One adult owns the stove and the hot caramel; children cut only soft fruit, supervised; individual cups and spoons for everyone.

photos

A separate, optional consent: no photos · group shots only · full permission. No consent — no camera. Surnames and schools never go public.

time

A dedicated timekeeper keeps the evening to 90 minutes; cleaning is planned in (30–45 minutes, team zones).
