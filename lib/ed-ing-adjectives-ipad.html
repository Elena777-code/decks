The rule · the twins

Things wear -ing. People feel -ed.

-ing — what the thing does to you

🎬The film is boring.
🎢The ride is exciting.

-ed — how the person feels

🥱I am bored.
🤩I am excited.

🧠 Ask one question: who is doing what? If the word describes the cause (the film, the trip, the lesson) — -ing. If it describes the person’s feeling — -ed. → The film bores ME → the film is boring, I am bored. The cause wears -ing, the feeling wears -ed.

The cause wears -ing, the feeling wears -ed — say it twice. The machine/dog test works too: a boring dog can’t bore itself on purpose; the -ed form always belongs to the feeler.
The twins · 1–4

Interest, boredom, excitement, tire

📚The book is interesting → I’m interested in history.
🎬The film is boring → I fell asleep — I was bored.
🎢The trip is exciting → I’m so excited about Sunday!
🥾The climb was tiring → my legs are tired.
Read each pair as a mini-story: the cause (-ing), then the feeler (-ed). She translates for herself — give her the time (Elena, 12.10).
The twins · 5–8

Surprise, fear, confusion, rest

🎁The present was surprising → I was surprised.
👻The noise was frightening → the cat was frightened.
🧩The map was confusing → we got confused and turned left.
🏖️The beach was relaxing → I felt relaxed for days.
The cat counts as a «person» here — animals feel -ed too. The map/confused pair is the school-life classic.
The twins · 9–12

Awe, annoyance, blush, drain

🏔️The view was amazing → we were amazed.
🦟The mosquito is annoying → I’m annoyed!
🙈The moment was embarrassing → my face went red — I was so embarrassed.
📦Moving day was exhausting → we were exhausted by ten.
exhausted = the strongest tired there is. The mosquito is small but wins the -ing.
The twins · 13–16

Letdown, terror, job done, shock

📉The ending was disappointing → I was disappointed.
🕷️The spider was terrifying → my little brother was terrified.
✅The cleanup was satisfying → I felt satisfied.
⚡The news was shocking → everybody was shocked.
terrifying/terrified = frightening/frightened, turned up to ten. satisfied = the quiet «job done» feeling — harder to picture, the sparkle helps.
The special one · the rebel of the family

Scary — scared

🎃 The film is scary → 😨 I’m scared!

Here the thing-word ends in -y, not -ing. The meaning is the same split: scary = the cause (the film, the dark, the spider), scared = the feeling (you, me, the cat).

🌙The dark basement is scary → don’t go there scared.
🐕The big dog is scary → the puppy is scared of it.
THE ONE REBEL — Elena asked to include it specially. The -y form replaces the -ing form for the cause; the -ed form (scared) behaves like all the others. frightened = scared, turned up slightly.
Your turn · new situations, the same twins

Say the word — then click

1Thirty maths questions in forty minutes. After the test, Dan was completely ______. → exhausted (the TEST was exhausting, DAN was exhausted)
2I dropped my phone in a silent classroom and everyone turned around. It was so ______. → embarrassing (the moment)
3The roller coaster goes upside down five times — it’s ______! Let’s ride again! → exciting (the ride)
4Grandpa’s mouth stayed open when we shouted «Surprise!» — he was ______. → amazed (Grandpa’s feeling)
5The instructions were in five languages and none of them made sense — totally ______. → confusing (the instructions)
6Warm sand, no phone, the sound of the sea — the whole week was ______. → relaxing (the week)
7The horror film? I couldn’t sleep — I was ______ all night. → scared (or frightened — the feeling)
8Nobody laughed at my joke. Nobody. How ______! → disappointing (the joke — and now I’m disappointed too)

The one question: is the word dressing the CAUSE or the FEELING?

NEW situations, same rules (the new-words-same-rules standing rule — never the teach sentences again). Item 8 has the twist: the joke is disappointing AND the speaker ends up disappointed — one situation, both twins.
Treasure task · read the clues

Which word fits Dan?

The school trip was three hours of tests, one after another. On the bus home, Dan’s eyes are closing and his pencil has fallen out of his hand.

Question: Dan is tiring — or Dan is tired? → tired — the trip (the cause) was tiring; Dan (the feeler) is tired. His falling pencil proves it.

The cause wears -ing. The feeler wears -ed — and Dan’s pencil just told us which one he is.

THE TREASURE TASK (standing): two true statements + one question — the answer is NOT in the statements, it follows from the rule (the deck-010 lesson). The falling pencil = the proof detail she can point at.
One line to remember

The cause wears -ing. The feeler wears -ed.

🎬The film, the trip, the test, the spider — -ing.
🥱You, me, Dan, the cat — -ed.
🎃…and the rebel: scary (the thing) — scared (the feeling).
