Present Perfect · the party game · WW4+

I've never told you… 🤫

Say your secret line with a straight face — and draw it on the paper face. The funniest face wins!

How to play

  1. Take a card (or deal five) and read it aloud: «I've never told you this, but…»
  2. Your friend must keep a straight face 😐 — no laughing allowed.
  3. Whatever happens, the line gets drawn on the paper face ✏️ — nose hair? draw nose hair!
  4. Next player. At the end — the funniest face wins 🏆.
✏️ The drawing goes on the PAPER face — never on real skin in lessons. (Camp can decide its own rules.)

👀 about you

🎫 Deal five cards

Click a card to open it, say it with your best poker face. Every deal = a fresh five!

🖨 The paper face — print one per player

Print this page's face (or trace it) — one big face per player, A4. Every confessed line = one doodle on your face. By the end of the round the faces tell the whole story 😂
