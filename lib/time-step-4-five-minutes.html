Teach · the fives

Each number = 5 minutes

The long hand counts in fives: 1 → five past, 2 → ten past, 3 → quarter past, 4 → twenty past…

THE ONE RULE: half the walk or less → PAST the hour that was. More than half → TO the hour that comes.

1Long hand on 2, short on 9 → … → ten past nine. 🔊
2Long hand on 4, short just after 7 → … → twenty past seven. 🔊
3Long hand on 10, short almost at 4 → … → ten to four — more than half the walk, so TO! 🔊
