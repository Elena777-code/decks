1. It's sunny in Vietnam. I'm ________ happy when it's sunny. always

2. I ________ get up at 7 o'clock — from Monday to Friday. usually

3. We ________ see the sun here in winter — only two or three times! hardly ever

4. I ________ drink coffee. I hate it! never

5. My sister ________ eats soup — maybe once a month. seldom

Word order reminder: I always brush my teeth · She never eats snails — the adverb lives BEFORE the verb, but AFTER am/is/are: He is always late.
