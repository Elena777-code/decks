Two Truths and a Lie 🔎

Three lines about you. Two are TRUE — one is a LIE. Find the lie!

🎬 Elena's example — try the class on this

click a card to accuse it first, or just reveal

🎲 Your turn — deal a round

One player gets the three cards. Two cards say TRUE, one secretly says LIE — the player builds their three lines to match (lie LAST, keep a straight face!). The class votes: which line is the lie? Then reveal.

🧱 Sentence frames to build your lines

Start with:
I'm…I've got…I can… I like…I once…My favourite … is… I never…I'm afraid of…
