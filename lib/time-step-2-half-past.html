Teach · half past

Half past — the long hand walks to 6

The long hand makes half a walk — from 12 down to 6. Half a walk = 30 minutes past the hour: half past.

… → It’s half past four. 🔊
… → It’s half past nine. 🔊

Look at the short hand — it sits HALF WAY between two numbers. It has left the old hour but not reached the new one: we still name the old hour. … → 12:30 → half past TWELVE — not one! 🔊

Treasure task 🗝

The film clock

Half past = 30 minutes. The film starts at half past six. It is six o’clock now.
How many minutes before the film? … → Thirty minutes! 🔊
