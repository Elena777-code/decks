Step 2 · the magic y

noun + y = adjective ✨

noun = a thing

rain · sun · wind · cloud · fog — you can point at it!

adjective = describes

What is it like? rainy · sunny · windy · cloudy · foggy

rain→rainy wind→windy cloud→cloudy sun→sunny fog→foggy
⚠ double the letter: sunny · foggy

rain → ? · sun → ? · fog → ? — say them ALL!

Step 3 · why adjectives?

An adjective paints a noun 🎨

a rainy day ☔ a sunny sky ☀️ a windy sea 🌊 a foggy morning 🌫️ a cloudy night ☁️
1The adjective stands BEFORE the noun: a rainy day — not “a day rainy”.
2One noun can “wear” different adjectives: a sunny day · a rainy day · a foggy day.

Your pair: “a ___ day” — what's the weather like today?
