    Teacher's notes

    How to play «Not in the list»

    1 ·N pictures on the screen — words the class already knows. This is early practice, not the teaching. The cards carry no words — the words live only in the list.
    2 ·Click «list ▾» — a list of 4 words appears. One thing from the screen is NOT in it.
    3 ·Click «list ▾» again — the list hides. Or «list · 1 sec ⏱» — it hides itself after a second. Try both — we keep the one you like.
    4 ·The pupil SAYS the thing that was not in the list. Check: tap that picture — right → ✓ glow, wrong → shake. Then «new round ↻».
    5 ·Two sets to try: 🏫 In the classroom (a bin, a clock, a chair, a desk, a board) · 🎨 Colours. Sets are swappable to any module's words; the list carries the articles (a bin).
    6 ·Universal game — every group, every language load. Status: in the Games section on the site.
