grammar · the box

be going to — plans 📌

who + am / is / are + going to + verb
I am going to swim .
You · We · They are going to play football .
He · She · It is going to ride a pony .
clickShe is going to ride a pony in the summer.
clickThey are going to swim in the sea.

⚠️ the verb never changes: he is going to ride — no -s!

grammar · negative

➖ not going to

I am not going to play tennis .
He · She · It is not (isn’t) going to cry .
You · We · They are not (aren’t) going to climb a tree .
clickThe boy isn’t going to cry. 😢
clickThey aren’t going to climb a tree. 🌳
grammar · questions

❓ Am / Is / Are … ?

1 · clickAre you going to swim in the sea? — Yes, I am. / No, I’m not.
2 · clickIs she going to ride a pony? — Yes, she is. / No, she isn’t.
3 · click❓ What are you going to do? — I’m going to watch TV.
4 · click🎵 But what am I going to do in the summer, without you?
