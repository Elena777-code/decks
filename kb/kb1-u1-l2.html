Kid's Box 1 (New Generation) · Unit 1 · Lesson 2 · KB1.2 — Thu 14:30

New toy friends & counting 1–10

Aim: by the end, the ss have named the new characters (Monty, Maskman, Marie) and counted objects from 1 to 10.

Language: What's your name?; counting 1–10; fingers; hands; dots; join  ·  New characters: Monty, Maskman, Marie

  • character flashcards — Star family + Monty, Maskman, Marie
  • number flashcards 1–10 (hung around the room for the Touch game)

Warm up · 10 min — part 1

Hello! — song & 'How are you?'

Greet the ss shaking hands. Play the song 'Hello!' (Super Simple Songs) and TPR all the actions in the video.

🎬 SONG — 'Hello!' (greeting + feelings, Super Simple Songs)

The конспект links the rutube copy; this is the same song — the local file from the Lesson 2 folder.

Show the 'How are you' flashcards one by one, repeat the phrases. Encourage the ss to pick one card and answer "How are you today?" Help them with TPR.

Cards: good · great · wonderful · tired · hungry · not so good. Board demo of the cards: slide 3 of the child deck (kb1-2-demo-002).

Warm up · 10 min — part 2

Puppets — 'Simon, how are you?'

Hand out the masks / puppets of the Star family and the 'How are you' flashcards.

Ask: "Simon, how are you?" — help the ss reply in role: "I'm great!", "I'm tired!" Repeat for the other characters.

A silly voice per character keeps them all answering. Board support: the child deck has the feelings cards on slide 3.

TPR · 6 min

Stand up! Sit down!

The ss sit on the chairs / floor. Say "Stand up" and TPR the action. Say "Sit down" — they mime it.

Hand out the Star family flashcards. Call: "Mrs Star, stand up!" · "Suzy, sit down!" — the ss with that card act it out. Repeat for all the characters.

Speed the calls up towards the end — it becomes a listening game (who catches their name?).

Presentation · 6 min

These are toys! — Monty, Maskman, Marie

Show a ball, a doll, a puzzle: "Look! These are toys." Stick the Star family on the board — elicit the names.

Add the new characters: introduce Monty, Maskman and Marie (masks/flashcards). Say: "They are toys!"

Monty (the mouse) · Maskman (the superhero) · Marie (with the glasses)

Say and mime the toy-box chant with the box:

📦 "Toys in the toy box" — put them in, close your eyes · ✨ "Come alive" — open your eyes, get the toys out · 🚶 "Walk and talk" — walk, TPR bla-bla-bla · 5️⃣ "On the count of 5" — show 5 fingers, count 1–5 on the fingers.

Repeat — the ss do the actions with you. This routine opens every lesson from now on.

Practice · 5 min

PB p.5 ex.1 — listen & do the actions

Hand out the flashcards / masks and repeat the chant together.

PB p.5 ex.1. Say: "Look! What do you see?" Elicit the names — the new characters are labelled in the picture. Point and repeat all the characters.

🎧 AUDIO — PB p.5 ex.1 (track 4)

Play the recording and mime the actions. Pause. The ss open their eyes, look in the book: "Now look and point" (TPR 'point'). Play the rest.

Track numbering verified against the icons on PB p.5 (ex.1 = ④). Pause the audio before flipping the slide — audio stops on slide change.

Production (speaking) · 6 min

What's your name? — in role

Invite 2 ss to the front. Give one a flashcard. Ask: "What's your name?" — they look at the card and say the character's name. Repeat with the second student.

Now they make a short dialogue in pairs. Help and assist if necessary — drill "What's your name?" first if needed.

ICQs: "Do you say YOUR name — I'm Katya/Sasha? Or the character's name — Simon/Suzy?"

Board support: slide 9 of the child deck shows the model dialogue (question → click → the character answers).

Math · 17 min — 1/4

Let's count fingers!

Say: "Show me your hand" (make sure the hand goes up). "Look! Fingers! Let's count!" — count 1–5. Then: "Show me 2 hands! Let's count fingers!" — count 1–10 together.

🎬 SONG — 'How many fingers?' (Super Simple Songs)

The ss look, listen and TPR the actions. As a stirrer: everyone stands up, does the actions and counts.

Math · 17 min — 2/4

PB p.5 ex.2 — the numbers chant

PB p.5 ex.2. "What can you see?" Elicit numbers; point and ask: "What number is it?" Invite volunteers to count 1–10.

🎧 AUDIO — PB p.5 ex.2 (track 5)

TPR "Listen and point" → play the recording, the ss point to the numbers. Play it again — they chant.

Before the second listening introduce "Say" (TPR): first loudly, then whispering — "Listen and say."

ICQs: "Do you say names: Simon/Stella? Do you say numbers: one, two, three?"

Math · 17 min — 3/4

How many fingers? + the game 'Touch'

Hold up different numbers of fingers — "How many fingers?" — the ss elicit the numbers.

Game 'Touch' (stirrer): the number flashcards hang around the classroom. Call: "Touch 1! Touch 4!" — the ss find the number, run and touch it.

Calm version: ask each student one by one and elicit the number before they go.

Math · 17 min — 4/4

AB p.5 — what's next? · join the dots

AB p.5, the character rows ('Look, match and say'). Elicit the names in the first row: "Marie, Maskman, Maskman, Monty, Marie, Maskman — WHAT'S NEXT?"

Maskman! — then match the row to the answer on the right.

"Row number 2! Look! How many Suzys?" — repeat for all the rows. Praise every count.

AB p.5, 'Join the dots'. "Take a pencil" (TPR). "Join the dots" — demonstrate on the board; elicit DOTS. ICQ: "What do you join?" — "The dots!" Say: "Listen and join the dots!" — play the recording and monitor.

⚠ Two catches, checked against the books: (1) the конспект calls the rows task "AB p.5 ex 3", but on the page it is ex.1 ('Look, match and say'; the dots task is ex.2) — same task, different number. (2) The local AB audio for Unit 1 has no p.5 track (only pp. 4, 6, 7) — pull the 'join the dots' recording from the classroom CD / the firm's audio. The dots join 1–10 in order and picture a toy with a smiley face in the middle.

Hand out the papers: "Draw 5 dots" — demonstrate as you draw. Then "Take your pencils… draw 2 dots… draw dots!" — guide the ss to draw different numbers of dots.

Feedback & goodbye · 5 min

One new word

Ask the ss to name 1 new word from this lesson. Go round everyone.

Board support: slide 13 of the child deck has the word chips — a tap reveals them, the ss shout theirs first.

Goodbye routine: the toy-box chant one more time (📦✨🚶5️⃣) — then the reading slot as usual.
