Warm up · 7 min

Draw a face!

Greet the ss shaking hands. Ask: "How are you?"

Hand out the face-shaped papers. Introduce 'face' (point to your face) and demonstrate 'draw' on the board.

ICQs: "Do you draw a face? Do you draw my face? Do you draw YOUR face?"

The ss draw happy / sad / tired faces. Walk and ask: "Is it happy? Is it sad?"

📏 Printouts issued today get glued into the copybooks the moment they are finished — say it BEFORE handing anything out.
Reading · phonics — 10 min · part 1

Phonics A a — apple, ant, ax, alligator

Present the sound /æ/ with realia: "Look! An apple! A — /æ/ — apple!" The ss repeat the sound several times.

Write on the board: 'A' — capital, 'a' — lower case.

📏 FIRST: glue worksheet Aa into the copybook! Then work on it.

Worksheet Aa, step by step:

1️⃣ Learn the words: an apple · an ant (pictures) + an ax · an alligator (word cards) · 2️⃣ Underline 'a' in an apple, an ant · 3️⃣ Color the circles: capital A = red, small a = blue · 4️⃣ Write the letters over the dots.

Board support: the child deck, slide 4 — the four word cards, click to name.

Reading · phonics — 10 min · part 2

The maze — follow the a's! 🏁

Worksheet Aa, the maze: "Follow the path and find all the a's." Point to the start: "Is it capital A or small a?" Demonstrate the first line.

ICQs: "Do you follow small a's or capital A's? Point to the start! Point to the finish!"

You have 2 minutes — set the timer! 🏁 Team race: table vs table — "Team 1… ready? Go!"

Board support: the child deck, slide 6 — the a-path to check together. Winner team gets the applause.

Reading · phonics — 10 min · part 3

Phonics B b — bag, bat, bed, bus + What's missing

Present /b/ with realia: "B — bag!" (show a real bag). Write 'B' capital, 'b' lower case.

📏 Glue worksheet Bb into the copybook!

Worksheet Bb, the same way: words (a bag · a bat + a bed · a bus) → cross-match → color the circles → trace the letters.

Game 'What's missing' with the new words — all 8. Team vs team: call a table number, that team closes their eyes, hide a card — "What's missing?"

Board support: the child deck, slide 8 — tap a card and it disappears.

Story — 15 min · part 4

Act it out! 🎭

Masks on! Play the story again — the ss listen and role-play. Pause after each line so they can repeat (shadowing).

Three groups: Marie · Maskman · Monty — each group says its part and acts.

Groups of three act out the whole story. Confident groups perform for the class — the tables are the stages! 🎬

Less confident ss take Monty (one line — "Look — a blue pencil!").
