🎵 Class management — Genki English style

Stand up! Sit down! · Rock, paper, scissors! ✊✋✌️

The commands chant — faster and faster! "Stand up! Sit down! Hands up! Hands down! Pencil up!" ✏️

Rock, paper, scissors — team battle: pair off, chant "Rock… paper… scissors… look and see!" — the winner's team presents the story first! 🏆

Win: "I can do it!" 💪 · Lose: "Try again!" 🔁

Story — 15 min · part 4

Act it out! 🎭

Masks on! Play the story again — the ss listen and role-play. Pause after each line so they can repeat (shadowing).

Three groups: Marie · Maskman · Monty — each group says its part and acts.

Groups of three act out the whole story. Confident groups perform for the class — the tables are the stages! 🎬

Less confident ss take Monty (one line — "Look — a blue pencil!"). The Genki winners present first.
