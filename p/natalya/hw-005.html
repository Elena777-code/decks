🪞 Repair your sentences — three from your own talk on Thursday.

Say your fix out loud first — then open the check.

“But more sentences was not clear.”
say your fix, then check
→ But more sentences were not clear. — “more sentences” is plural: were (or “weren't clear”).
“I had not time enough to watch the video.”
say your fix, then check
→ I didn't have enough time to watch the video. — “enough” sits before the noun: enough time · enough money. (After an adjective it follows: clear enough, good enough.)
“It was clear for me.”
say your fix, then check
→ It was clear to me. — clear to somebody: The answer was clear to everyone.
