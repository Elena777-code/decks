🔧 The new grammar — have something done.

From our lesson: you don't do it yourself — a specialist does it FOR you. have (in any form) + the thing + the 3rd form:

I had my face done at the Institute of Beauty.
I am having my house decorated right now.
Natalya has her face done every two years.

Write five sentences of your own — five things people do for you:

я подстриглась (в салоне)
the pattern
→ I had my hair cut (at the salon). — cut · cut · cut!
нам починили машину
the pattern
→ We had our car repaired.
нам доставляют еду
the pattern
→ We have our food delivered. — your own example from our lesson!
мне сделали маникюр
the pattern
→ I had my nails done. — сделали: the completed act, the past. (the habit: Мне делают каждую неделю → I have my nails done.)
квартиру убирают за нас
the pattern
→ We have the flat cleaned.

✍️ Write YOUR five — your face, your car, your flat, your meals, your nails — anything real. Bring them to the lesson.
