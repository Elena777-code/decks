The Fourteen Chips

Fourteen cards from yesterday’s lesson. Each card is a chip: answer it — and the chip is yours. 25 seconds each, which is genuinely generous. Type your answer, press Enter.

Miss a card, and the chip goes back to the bank — with the right answer shown, for your notebook. The order shuffles every round.
