The rule · two little verbs

IS or HAS GOT?

IS = what it IS 🪪
Anapa is a town.
It is in Russia.
It is famous and hot.
HAS GOT = what it HAS 🎒
It has got clean beaches.
It has got hotels and museums.
Anapa has got a big beach.
Anapa is a Russian town.
Anapa has got nineteen beaches!
The town is on the sea.
The sea has got clean water.

Phonics · two vowels go walking

ea — the first one talks 🗣

ea → E /iː/

When two vowels go walking, the first does the talking — it says its own name.

clean beach sea tea eat read teacher peach
“Each teacher on the clean beach eats a peach and drinks tea by the sea!”

Say it 3 times — faster each time. Teachers love clean beaches.
