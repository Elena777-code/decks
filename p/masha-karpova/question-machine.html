❓ The Question Machine

1 · The machine. Question = WH + do/does/did + subject + base verb. Where do we get the bus? · Does he play chess? · Did she like the film? The answer tells you the auxiliary: yes, she did → Did…? · yes, he does → Does…?
2 · The doer trap. When WHO (or WHAT) is the doer of the action — it IS the subject, so NO auxiliary: Mia invited Tom. → Who invited Tom? (never ~~Who did invite~~)
3 · The receiver returns it. When you ask about the receiver — the auxiliary comes back: Mia invited Tom. Ask about Tom → Who did Mia invite?

The trap from your drill: "Who invited Sam…" was offered when the question was about the RECEIVER. Ask yourself first: am I asking about the DOER or the RECEIVER?

Six questions to build. The answer is given — build the question that fits it. Type the whole question, press Enter. Missed ones come back first next time. 🔧

The British corner · just a smile, not a mark

🇬🇧 Keep the U!

Your colourful lost its U last week — that's the American spelling whispering. British keeps it: colour → colourful · favourite · flavour · harbour. When the keyboard suggests the American one — thank it politely, and type the British one anyway. 😉
