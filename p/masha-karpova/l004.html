The story in four frames

Two detectives, one criminal

🕵️‍♂️ Frame 1

Footprints, a magnifying glass, complaints…

What's happening? → The detectives are following footprints; one complains about his magnifying glass and his job.

🥫 Frame 2

The detective finds something near the wall.

→ A clue: a can of spray paint!

🎨 Frame 3

The other detective is not impressed. Someone is standing very still by the wall…

→ "It's not a clue, it's rubbish." The criminal pretends to be part of the graffiti!

🚶 Frame 4

The detectives walk away.

→ They give up: the crime stays unsolved. The detectives are useless and complain a lot; the criminal is clever.

The patterns (with traps)

un- · im- · in- · il- · ir- — and exceptions

im- before /p/ → impossible, impolite · ir- before /r/ → irregular, irresponsible · il- before /l/ → illogical, illegal · un- — the big one: unhappy, unusual, unfriendly

⚠ But listen to the SOUND, not the rule: unpopular, unreliable, unlucky — /ʌ/ sound, not im-/ir-!

1Sam never says hello. He's ___. — un- · unfriendly
2A waiter who shouts is ___. — im- · impolite
3Homework about dinosaurs in an English lesson? ___. — ir- · irrelevant
4The doctor's handwriting is ___. — il- · illegible
