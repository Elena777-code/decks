sneaky — he takes the wheel when you're not looking.

The Panic Monster 😱

He lies dormant — asleep, harmless — until a deadline is near.
Then he wakes up and causes mayhem — total chaos.
Only he scares the monkey away.

The comfort zone

Your comfort zone = where everything is easy and safe. Growth lives just outside it — in the challenging experiences and the big emotions they bring.

Your turn: one moment in Abkhazia that was OUTSIDE your comfort zone — one perfect sentence with one emotion.

The debate

Agree or disagree — with Tim Urban

1Deadlines make people do their best work. — model: “Although deadlines stress me out, I agree: they wake the Panic Monster.”
2Everyone is a procrastinator — some just hide it better. — model: “Taking into account the monkey in every brain, I agree, though some systems hide it better.”
3Procrastination is always bad. — model: “Despite the mayhem it causes, sometimes a deadline-off project turns out fine.”

The rules of the game: every answer carries one contrast link (although / in spite of / though) — and try “Taking into account…”
