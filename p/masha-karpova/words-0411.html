📚 The seven words

  1. a license 🚗 · noun an official document that says you are allowed to do something — to drive, to own a dog. He's eighteen, but he hasn't got a driving license yet.
    You get a driving license when you pass the test.
    Russianводительские права; лицензия
  2. a neighbourhood 🏘 · noun the part of a town where you live — and the people who live there. The children in his neighbourhood didn't have money for school books.
    Everyone in our neighbourhood knows each other.
    Russianрайон; окрестность; соседи
  3. an intention 🎯 · noun what you plan and decide to do — a plan in your head. Her intention is to study abroad.
    I had no intention of hurting your feelings.
    Russianнамерение
  4. pity 💙 · noun (uncountable) a sad feeling for someone with problems — you feel sorry for them. He felt pity for the children who couldn't pay for school.
    What a pity the concert is cancelled!
    Russianжалость · what a pity = какая жалость
  5. embarrassed 😳 · adjective feeling uncomfortable or ashamed in front of people. I was very embarrassed when I forgot my lines in the school play.
    Stop laughing — she's already embarrassed.
    Russianсмущённый; испытывающий неловкость
  6. humiliating 😞 · adjective making someone feel very small and ashamed. The team's 0 : 10 defeat was humiliating.
    Being laughed at is humiliating.
    Russianунизительный
  7. to warm up 🔥 · verb to prepare your fingers, voice or body before playing sport or music. Musicians warm up before a concert.
    The players warmed up before the match.
    Russianразогреваться; разыгрываться
