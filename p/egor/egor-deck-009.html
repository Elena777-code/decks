the five exam answers · your facts, upgraded

Say it like this — your words inside

Where is Russia located?
Russia is situated in the eastern part of Europe and the northern part of Asia. It borders twelve countries on land, and it is washed by twelve seas — it even has a sea border with the USA.
What type of climate does Russia have?
Russia is the largest country in the world, so it has a wide range of climates: the north is cold and snowy for most of the year, but the summers in the south are very hot and dry.
What can you tell about famous Russian cities?
Moscow, the capital, is the largest city — over 12 million residents. And Sochi, a resort city on the Black Sea, hosted the 2014 Winter Olympics.
What are some traditional Russian foods?
Russian cuisine is rich and diverse: borscht — a soup made with beetroot — pelmeni filled with meat, and blini served with sour cream or caviar.
Music and literature?
One of the most famous Russian composers is Tchaikovsky — his ballets Swan Lake and The Nutcracker are famous all over the world. As for literature, Pushkin is the most famous Russian poet.
