brick 1 · the opening statement

Open like you mean it

Write YOUR version on paper first. Then click the model lines and compare — idea for idea, not word for word.

The table presents data on the reasons why teenagers in Zetland choose to study online. one sentence, what the table IS — nothing more
The most popular reason is the wish to have a more flexible schedule (42%), while the least popular one is the desire to feel more comfortable (7%). top + bottom in one line — brick 2 starts itself

bricks 2–3 · the facts and the comparison

Say the numbers the exam way

Never repeat «42%» twice the same way. The podcast taught you the register:

A sizable portion of the teenagers (42%) study online to have a more flexible schedule. a sizable portion = the big share
About a quarter of the respondents (24%) want more interesting tasks. 24% ≈ a quarter
A tiny proportion of them (7%) choose online education to feel more comfortable. a tiny proportion — the episode's own phrase
Almost three times fewer teenagers avoid peer pressure (15%) than seek a flexible timetable (42%). the comparison line — brick 3 in one sentence
It could be explained by the fact that teenagers value freedom and comfort in their studies. your comment after the comparison — YOUR idea, one line
🎯 two facts + one comparison + one comment — your words
brick 4 · the problem → why → solution

The brick you were missing

Your September essay named the problem and the solution — but never said WHY it is a problem. Ask yourself: why is it bad? what follows? — and one more sentence appears.

However, studying online has a drawback: many teenagers lack social interaction, which can make them feel isolated and lose motivation. problem + the WHY — «which can…» does the work
To solve this problem, they can join online discussion forums or study with friends by video call, which helps to maintain social connections. solution + its result — the chain closes
Another problem is poor time management: students who study online may miss deadlines and fall behind. Creating a structured schedule can help. the reserve problem — different essay, same brick
🎯 problem → why → solution → result: four steps, two sentences
brick 5 · your opinion, with style

Close like the podcast

In my opinion, online education will play an increasingly important role in the 21st century. the safe opening of the final brick
While online platforms certainly hold promise, there is a long track record suggesting that technology alone isn't enough. the balanced line — straight from the episode, essay-safe
Technology should augment, not replace, the teacher — and the heart of effective education will remain human connection. the quotable close — one line, top-band flavour

✍️ Pick ONE of the three closes — or build your own from their parts.
