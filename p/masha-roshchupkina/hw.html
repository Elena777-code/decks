Seven words from “Bright sparks!”

Write each word into your notebook: the translation in Russian or the meaning in English or one example. Write first — then click to check yourself.

  1. average
    check
    🇷🇺 средний; среднее значение · the typical amount or level in a group
    An average score in the test is 100 points. · The average child watches two hours of TV a day.
  2. influence
    check
    🇷🇺 влиять; влияние · to have an effect on someone or something (verb and noun)
    Diets and health influence the brain. · It has a big influence on my results.
  3. responsible
    check
    🇷🇺 ответственный · the person who must do something or who caused it — responsible FOR
    You are responsible for your own progress. · Who is responsible for this mess?
  4. report
    check
    🇷🇺 доклад; отчёт · a piece of writing that gives information about something
    A report from researchers in Scotland. · The weather report says rain.
  5. improve
    check
    🇷🇺 улучшать(ся); становиться лучше · to become better or make something better
    I want to improve my English. · Her results improved every year.
  6. improvements
    check
    🇷🇺 улучшения · the noun (improve → improvement): things that get better
    We have made improvements in diets and health. · The school made three improvements this year.
  7. change
    check
    🇷🇺 менять(ся); изменение · to become or make different (verb and noun)
    The tests changed — they became harder. · Test writers made changes every few years.

✍️ Your five sentences — repair them yourself

Say the corrected sentence aloud first — then click to check. These five open your next lesson.

  1. “I’m quite intelligent in literature.”
    check
    → I’m quite good at literature. — intelligent describes a mind, not a school subject; subjects take good at.
  2. “I feel confident when I write some poems.”
    check
    → I feel confident when I write poems. — a general habit, no some; and the full stop.
  3. “I’m hard working in revise Chinese.”
    check
    → I’m hard-working when I revise Chinese. — no second verb after in; when I revise carries it.
  4. “I’m talented at making models or crafting.”
    check
    → ✓ Perfect as it is. — talented at + -ing, exactly right.
  5. “I’m good at team working.”
    check
    → I’m good at teamwork. — one word, the noun.
