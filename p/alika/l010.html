Nina's New Ribbon 🎀

Nina is nine. She is a gymnast. 🤸‍♀️
She has got a new ribbon. It is pink and long! 🎀
Nina goes to the gym with her rope — it is new too! 🪢
“Why do you love the gym, Nina?” — “Because I can jump, turn and dance!”
At home her cat Smokey plays with the ribbon. 🐈‍⬛
“Why, Smokey, why?” — Nina laughs. “Because it is new!”
The ribbon flies! Smokey's eyes are big. 😻
