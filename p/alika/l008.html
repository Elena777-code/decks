Lily's Yellow Kite 🪁

Lily is nine. She has got a new kite. 🪁
The kite is yellow. Lily loves it!
“Why do you like it?” — “It can fly!”
In the park Lily runs. The kite flies in the sky!
Baby Tom sees it and cries. 😢
Mum gives him a new ice cream. 🍦
“How old is the kite?” — “It's new! It's one!”
Now the family is happy. 😊

Open the brackets! ✍️

🎵 «it, he, she — глагол с s-кой напиши!»

Example: She (like) pizza. — likes!  ·  I (like) pizza. — like!
1. Tom plays (play) tennis. ⚽
2. I like (like) gymnastics. 🤸‍♀️
3. Baby Tom cries (cry). 😢
4. We go (go) to school. 🏫
5. The kite flies (fly) in the sky. 🪁
6. My legs hurt (hurt). 🦵
7. She watches (watch) TV. 📺
8. They swim (swim) fast. 🏊
