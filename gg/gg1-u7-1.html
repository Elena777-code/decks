GG1 · Unit 7 · Lesson 7.1 · Teacher's script · SB pp 82–83

Animals — wild animals

Lesson aim (on the board)
I can talk about animals.
Vocabulary worksheet 7.1 printed (TB p. 151 · SSD: Go_Getter_1_Photocopiables.ru/Unit_7)
Audio ready: 3.16 (listen & repeat) · 3.17 (animal sounds) — Class Audio на диске или интерактивная книга
Board + markers for the team score (пригодится и для викторины в следующий раз)
Homework to write on the board at the end: Workbook p. 62
Step 1 · Warm-up · ~5 min · books closed

Guess the day → predict the topic

  • One student secretly chooses a day of the week and says what he/she does on it (I have two lessons in the afternoon…). The class guesses the day. Then in pairs.
  • Write ANIMALS on the board → pairs predict what they will learn today.
Say
Think of one day. What do you do on that day? — Now guess: what day is it? … Today — ANIMALS. What will we learn?
Step 2 · Lead-in · ~3 min · books closed

Brainstorm wild animals

  • The class names wild animals — you write them on the board.
  • If a word is unknown to the others — the student draws it on the board.
Say
Wild animals — name them! I'll write them on the board. Don't know the word? Draw it!
everything stays on the board — return to it in the Finishing step
Step 3 · Exercise 1 · ~5 min · pairs · SB p. 82

Match photos and words

  • Pairs write the number of each photo (1–7) next to the word.
  • Model first: point to a word + photo, say "I know that!" with a thumbs-up — they continue.
  • One photo is extra — not in the word list.
Say
Open your books at page 82. Seven photos, seven words — write the numbers. Which photo is extra?
The 7 words elephantmonkeytigergiraffelioncrocodilekangaroo
Step 4 · Exercise 2 · ~5 min · 🔊 audio 3.16

Listen and repeat

  • Play 3.16 — pause after each word, the class repeats.
  • A student finds the extra photo and names the animal:
Extra photo = № 2 — a whale
  • Critical thinking: what does wild mean? Why?
Say
Listen and repeat. … Which photo is extra? What animal is it? … Why "wild"? — not a pet, lives free.
weak class: they point at every animal on pp 82–83 while repeating
Step 5 · Exercise 3 · ~5 min · pairs race · SB p. 83

Complete the words

  • Pairs race: finish the gapped words with the Vocabulary box.
  • Check: pull lollipop sticks (палочки с именами) — the student says AND spells the answer.
Say
Finish the words. Work in pairs — ready, steady, go! … (имя!), spell "snake"!
Vocabulary box — 15 wild animals birdbutterflycrocodileelephantfishflyfroggiraffekangaroolionmonkeysnakespidertigerwhale
Step 6 · Exercise 4 · ~5 min · 🔊 audio 3.17

Animal sounds

  • Play 3.17 — students listen and number the words (a / b / c).
  • Weak class: pause after each sound, discuss, then answer.
  • Then reverse: you name an animal — they make the sound!
Say
Listen. What animal is it? Number the words a, b, c. … Now — a lion! What's the sound?
The 3 words a · birdb · elephantc · frog
Step 7 · Exercise 5 · ~5 min · individually · SB p. 83

Read and circle

  • Individual work: circle the correct animal in each sentence.
  • They justify answers pointing to the photos of the lesson.
  • Weak students: revise action verbs — Vocabulary box, SB p. 59 (Unit 5).
1I can fly! — spider / bird
2I can swim! — whale / butterfly
3I'm very tall! — frog / giraffe
4I can't jump! — kangaroo / elephant
5I've got big teeth! — crocodile / fly
6I've got arms and legs. — snake / monkey
Say
Read and circle. … Why? Point to the photo.
Step 8 · Exercise 6 · ~4 min · SB p. 83

The names got mixed!

  • Half-words are jumbled (ti · butter · kanga · croco · li · mon …) — students write the correct names.
  • Challenge the strong ones: Vocabulary box closed!
  • Different students write answers on the board.
Say
The animal names got mixed! Write the correct names. No Vocabulary box if you're brave!
tiger · butterfly · kangaroo · crocodile · lion · monkey
Step 9 · Exercise 7 · ~8 min · pairs · creative

Fantasy animals

  • Pairs draw three fantasy animals — partner names them (a giraffe + a bird = a "giraffird"!).
  • Time limit 4–5 min — keeps focus.
  • Pairs comment on each other's animals; then each pair draws one on the board — the class guesses the name.
Say
Draw three fantasy animals! What is it? — It's a giraffe and a bird: a "giraffird"! … Time's up in 1 minute.
Extra · if time / high energy · ~5 min · teams

Pictionary — a taste of team games

  • Two teams (or groups of four). One artist per turn comes to the board.
  • You whisper the animal to the artist — they draw, no words.
  • The team that guesses first wins a point. In fours: the artist rotates every round.
Say
Teams! One artist… Come here — your animal is… (whisper) Draw! Don't speak!
Это репетиция: в следующий раз — викторина-загадки по командам (It's big. It's grey. It can run. → elephant). Правила: Library/Bank-Pool/Team Quiz/team-quiz.md
Step 10 · Finishing the lesson · ~4 min

Self-check

  • Students look at their warm-up predictions and circle the new words they learnt today (in the Vocabulary box).
  • Ask: "Can you talk about animals?" — they show self-assessment cards (😊 / 😐 / ☹).
  • They copy the objective "I can talk about animals" into notebooks + draw the emoticon of their progress.
Say
Circle the NEW words you learnt today. Can you talk about animals? Show me!
