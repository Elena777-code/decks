Ten of these words are Thursday's dictation — meet them properly! 📝

The TB's new-language lists for pp. 14–15: narrow escape · hills · breathing (n) · slow down · erupt · certain · (only) chance · flash · destroy · lava · ash(es) + warn · bright (p. 15). Quick glosses: narrow escape = got away, but only just · breathing = the air going in and out · slow down = become slower · certain = sure · chance = the one opportunity · flash = an instant · destroy = break completely · ashes = what is left after the fire · warn = tell about danger · bright = full of light. The DICTATION list is ten: narrow escape · erupt · lava · ashes · destroy · chance · certain · warn · bright · breathing (hills, slow down and flash rest — they come free in sentences). 3 minutes — they meet the words again in the WB exercises.
