The rule of the day — the board
HAVE TO
HAD TO

have to = it is necessary · had to = it was necessary (the past)

The rule · have to / has to · had to

One rule — three shapes 🧩

HAVE TO / HAS TO — now

I / you / we / they → have to
he / she / it → has to

+ the verb as it is (no -s, no -ed!):
She has to wear a helmet.

HAD TO — the past

everyone → had to

yesterday · last weekend · in 2020…
We had to wait for the bus.

? and ✗

Do / Does / Did … have to…?
Do you have to take a test?
don't / doesn't / didn't + have to
You don't have to bring any water.
