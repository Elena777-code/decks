You want to buy an exotic pet: a snake 🐍, a tropical fish 🐠, a spider 🕷 or a lizard 🦎. Think:

1how much it costs
2if it's dangerous
3where to keep it — how much space?
4what to feed it — how often?

Someone wants to buy an exotic pet. You tell them:

1how much it is
2if it's dangerous or not
3where to keep it — how big the tank should be
4how often and what to feed it
5if it is safe to play with the pet
