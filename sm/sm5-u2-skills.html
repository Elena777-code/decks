Reading · SB p. 29 · the magazine article

The lost tribe 🏹

Read the article and answer the questions — then justify: Why? How do you know?

1Were the men from the tribe happy to see the plane? No! They appear angry — bows and arrows at the plane.
2Were the scientists surprised to see the tribe? No — «The scientists were there to find this lost tribe».
3Were the scientists happy to see the tribe? Yes — «…they wanted to show it to the world».
4Were the men from the tribe surprised to see the plane? Yes — they live without gadgets or electricity. A plane is a big surprise!

The new words — click a word, get the meaning!

1tribe a group of people who live together — their own rules, their own language
2hidden nobody can find it
3passenger a person travelling in a car, a plane…
4bow and arrow a weapon — you shoot arrows
5make sure to check that something is true
6illness when you are ill — a sickness
7outside world all the places and people far away from you
