How to SAY a big number 🔢

100 = a hundred 1,000 = a thousand 1,000,000 = a million

Two rules: break the number at every comma · say and only after hundred!

500five hundred
999nine hundred and ninety-nine
25,000twenty-five thousand
130,500one hundred and thirty thousand, five hundred
250,000two hundred and fifty thousand
