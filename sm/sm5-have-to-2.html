Grammar · don't have to

Don't have to = it's OK! 🎈

HAVE TO — it is necessary ✅

You have to do the dishes.
nobody else will!

DON'T HAVE TO — it's OK not to 🎈

You don't have to love it!
the song's own words — nobody must love their chores

Say it with don't have to — then check!

1It's Sunday tomorrow. (get up early) I don't have to get up early!
2The dishwasher is full. (wash the dishes) I don't have to wash the dishes!
3School's closed today. (go to school) I don't have to go to school!
4Dad is driving us. (take the bus) We don't have to take the bus!
5It's the holidays! (do homework) I don't have to do homework!
