vocabulary skill · understanding suffixes: -ly · book p. 11

The -ly machine 🤖

🤖 Add -ly to an adjective → you get an ADVERB (how you do it!):

quick + ly = quickly frequent + ly = frequently real + ly = really
1. My usual breakfast is cereal. → I usually have cereal for breakfast.
2. My parents are slow eaters. → My parents eat slowly.
3. It is rare for me to eat junk food. → I rarely eat junk food.
4. I have a quick meal in the evening. → I eat quickly in the evening.
5. My sister is a professional cook. → My sister cooks professionally.

say the adverb first — then click to check!
